# 0.2.3 · Nothing left out
date: 2026-11-10

## A tone keeps everything you said
Professional used to make a message polite by leaving parts of it out. Now your complaint, your apology, your thanks, your "I know you're busy" and your "maybe" all stay, put in calm, professional words of its own. Friendly, Shorter and Clearer keep more of what you wrote too.

# 0.2.2 · A tidier desk
date: 2026-11-10

## Updates and About, each on one page
Updates shows the version you have with the button to check right beside it, over the shelf of versions. About has who made Iris, the facts about your copy, and every link in one place. Neither needs scrolling.

- The small line of words up the side of Settings sits lower, away from the list of pages.
- Quick Fix leaves the model asleep where Iris is off.
- Your clipboard comes back, even from a slow app.
- The menu bar menu always opens on its commands.
- After Set up later, nothing downloads until you're back.
- A model changed mid-check offers Try again.

# 0.2.1 · A careful hand
date: 2026-11-10

## Quick Fix asks before a big cut
When a fix would drop a good part of what you wrote, Iris shows it to you first instead of replacing your text.

## Your commas and spacing stay yours
Iris changes only what it fixed. Numbers like 1,000, links, lists and the spacing in the rest of your text come back exactly as you typed them.

## Fix takes your sentence as it is
A sentence like "the user wants dark mode" or "please provide the invoice" is corrected like any other.

## Your clipboard stays yours
What you copied is still there after you press the keys twice or in an empty box. If the app you were writing in has gone, Iris keeps the fix on your clipboard for you to paste, and never pastes it somewhere else.

## The pen follows your keyboard
Move through Iris with Tab and a pen ring shows which switch, checkbox, choice or picker you're on, the same ring the buttons have.

- Quick Fix replaces your text in apps where Iris reads it by copying what you selected.
- If you chose the small message for text with nothing to fix, you get it every time, also for text Iris has checked before.
- A message that starts with "Output:" or "Corrected:" keeps those words, and a list numbered i. keeps its small i.
- Iris catches a misspelled first word on a new line.
- VoiceOver says what Iris is doing when you open its menu, names each choice in Settings, and says which model suits this Mac during setup.
- If an update stops part way or the connection stalls, Iris tells you in plain words and you can try again.
- Your shortcuts work again as soon as you click away from choosing new keys.

# 0.2.0 · Turn the pages
date: 2026-11-09

## Every version, a book to open
See what changed in each version of Iris by opening it like a little book: turn the pages, one change to a panel, and close it when you're done.

## Browse every version with a swipe
Swipe sideways over the shelf, or click older and newer under it, to glide through every version of Iris. Let go and the nearest one comes forward with what's in it.

## Buttons you can feel
The main button on every screen answers your pointer and your click, and shows clearly where you are when you move with the keyboard.

- VoiceOver reads only the pages of a book that are open in front of you.
- Arrows on buttons and links show which way each one takes you, and links that open your browser are marked ↗.
- Every Settings page fits the window, however little is on it.
- Update & reopen installs the newest Iris every time, and opens on it.
- Fix leaves a phrase that was already right, like "bold with your ideas", the way you wrote it.
- Iris reads what you're writing on a web page in Brave and browsers like it, such as a post on X, without you selecting it first. The first time after the browser opens can take a few seconds.

# 0.1.0 · First print
date: 2026-11-07

## Fix and four tones
Fix corrects grammar and spelling and keeps your words. Professional, Friendly, Shorter and Clearer are one key away.

## Quick Fix
Fixes the text box you're in and replaces it straight away. Big rewrites open the panel so you can look first.

## Words Iris never changes
Names, product terms and jargon stay exactly as you wrote them. Put a word back and Iris learns it.

## Your style, your apps
Your own tones and style, a starting tone for each app, and Iris turned off where it shouldn't read.

## Runs on this Mac
The model runs here with Apple's MLX, and works offline once it's downloaded.

- Writing in Hinglish or another language? Fix turns it into English and shows your original beside it.
- The flower in the menu bar shows what Iris is doing.
- Iris frees the model's memory when you haven't used it for a while.
- Setup in three steps: allow, get the model, try it.
